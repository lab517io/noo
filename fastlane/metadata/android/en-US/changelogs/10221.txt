Find opens as a dialog listing the matches; Enter opens one with the found text centred and marked. Preferences → Appearance adds a Classic style, ten accent colours and, on desktops, the system title bar. A task's context menu starts and stops tracking and opens its timeline. Register a sync account from Preferences → Sync, which also shows how much of the account's storage is used. The Linux AppImage runs on Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12, and on systems with a newer GStreamer.
